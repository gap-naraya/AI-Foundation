# CLAUDE.md — Level 3 AI Assessment Evidence

## Primary Reference
**Authoritative Document:** `AI Impact Evaluation for MANAGERS.pdf` (in this folder)

This PDF defines the official Level 3 evaluation criteria. Use it as the source of truth for all Level 3 work.

## The 5 Evaluation Dimensions

When working with evidence in this folder, always reference these dimensions from the PDF:

### 1. AI Integration in Your Daily Management Workflow
- AI is the default starting point for management workflows
- Repeatable, designed processes (not ad-hoc tool usage)
- Time reclaimed from admin work reinvested in strategy, relationships, team development

### 2. Appropriate Use of AI and Validation of Outputs
- Human-in-the-loop architecture maintained
- Models exemplary validation behavior for the organization
- Contributes to defining responsible use practices
- Understands where AI should not replace human judgment

### 3. Productivity and Measurable Impact on Delivery Outcomes
- Demonstrates measurable improvement across **multiple** metric categories
- Freed capacity used strategically to launch initiatives or deepen relationships
- Evidence grounded in real before/after metrics

### 4. Capacity to Design and Orchestrate AI-Driven Processes
- Designs and leads multi-step AI-powered workflows
- Combines two or more AI tools into integrated systems
- Builds or architects automated pipelines for recurring processes
- Creates reusable blueprints adopted by other managers or teams

### 5. Use of AI as a Multiplier Across Teams and the Organization
- Systematically uplevels direct reports' AI maturity
- Creates AI-enhanced frameworks adopted by others
- Influences organizational AI strategy beyond own portfolio
- Contributes to L&D content or mentors managers

## Evidence Standards

All evidence files in this folder must meet these criteria:

✅ **Grounded in REAL examples** — No hypothetical scenarios  
✅ **Measurable metrics** — Quantified before/after where applicable  
✅ **Demonstrated impact** — Not just tool usage, but strategic outcomes  
✅ **Mapped to dimensions** — Each piece addresses one or more of the 5 dimensions  
✅ **Validated against PDF** — Ensure it meets Level 3 standard, not Level 2

## When Working with Agents

If spawning agents to work in this folder, they should:
1. Read this CLAUDE.md first
2. Reference the PDF for evaluation criteria
3. Extract evidence grounded in Nelson's actual work (not hypothetical)
4. Map all evidence to the 5 dimensions
5. Validate against Level 3 standards before finalizing
