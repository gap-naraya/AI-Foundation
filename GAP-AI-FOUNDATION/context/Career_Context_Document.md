## 3. Core Project Structural Deficiencies
* **Technical Vacuum:** The project lacks a consistent Software/Solution Architect to evaluate and drive the technical solution, creating severe technical leadership gaps for the fully allocated team.
* **Product Owner Absenteeism:** The Product Owner is only involved in specific, isolated periods of development rather than deeply embedded in the day-to-day lifecycle, leading to scope volatility and high execution uncertainty.
* **The Operational Strain:** Because of these two gaps, you are forced to absorb the high uncertainty of the work needed, balancing scope containment without the structural support of a dedicated PO or Architect.

---

## 4. Superpowers & Underutilized Levers
* **Elite Analytical Thinking:** The ability to look at complex, moving project parts, identify core risks, and formulate mitigation strategies before they impact delivery. 
* **World-Class Interpersonal Skills:** A peerless ability to build deep trust, foster self-managed team environments, maintain high morale, and build functional bridges in both Spanish and English.
* **Technical Integration:** Unlike many pure program managers, possesses the unique ability to dive deep into the technical side of the project alongside engineers, earning deep technical respect.
* **The Underutilization:** Currently, these elite analytical and people skills are being bottlenecked by routine administrative data-chasing and reading out daily status reports for upper management.

## 5. Friction, Vulnerabilities & Energy Drains
* **The Ultimate Drain:** Delivering bad news and navigating reactive C-level escalations from the client. 
* **The Core Regret / Lesson:** Moving forward under inaccurate initial assumptions and not pushing back on upper management with enough "character" during the flawed initiation phase of the project to demand architect allocation and PO commitment.
* **Current State:** Feeling structurally stuck in a poorly initiated project while simultaneously feeling under the microscope for future internal promotions.

## 6. Future Trajectory & Strategic Desires (The 2-Year Horizon)
* **Primary Goal:** Stability, account reassignment, or promotion to **Delivery Manager**. 
* **The Strategic Shift:** Desires to manage project scope more aggressively and enforces strict Product Owner involvement throughout the entire development lifecycle, rather than just periodic check-ins.
* **The Ideal Environment:** A role or account where project boundaries are healthy, upper management values proactive risk-escalation, and success is built on accurate, data-driven planning rather than reactive fire-fighting.
