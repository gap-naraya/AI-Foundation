## Part 2: The Tactical Execution Plan

### ⏱️ The Time Budget (The 6-Hour Sacrifice)
To hit Level 3 without dropping the ball on your daily work, you are blocking out 6 hours per week of personal time:
* Tuesday: 1 Hour
* Thursday: 2 Hours
* Weekend: 3 Hours
* The Rule: If work steals a weekday hour, you must pay it back over the weekend.

### 🛡️ Preventing the "Freeze" (Analysis Paralysis)
* **The Problem:** When things go wrong, worrying about your job makes you overthink, which lowers your analytical skills and paralyzes you.
* **The Rule of 45:** You are not allowed to sit alone staring at an "unknown" problem for more than 45 minutes. 
* **The Solution:** After 45 minutes of being stuck, stop overthinking and ask your manager, Gerardo, for help.

## Part 3: Communication Scripts for Gerardo

Since Gerardo is your manager and your Level 3 mentor, use these templates to get help quickly without looking like you are panicking.

### 💬 Option #1: The "Unlocking an Unknown" Script (Your Primary Weapon)
*Copy and paste this to Gerardo when you hit a technical blocker or a freeze:*

Hi Gerardo,

I’m working through [Component/Issue Name] on the Mediquant DOM project. To ensure we keep our timeline on track, I want to sync with you briefly on an unknown variable regarding [the specific technical gap or requirement].

I’ve mapped out two potential approaches, but I want to get your perspective to ensure we make the right call quickly. Do you have 10 minutes today or tomorrow morning to help me unblock this?

### 💬 Option #2: The "Priority Check" Script
*Use this when the project gets chaotic and you need to clear the noise:*

Hi Gerardo,

We have a few moving parts on the Mediquant project right now, and I want to make sure my analytical focus is directed exactly where the company needs it most today.

Currently, I am prioritizing [Task A] and [Task B]. Can we do a quick 5-minute alignment check to ensure these match your top priorities for the week?

### 💬 Option #3: The "Mentorship" Script
*Use this during calm 1-on-1s to show him you are working toward your Level 3:*

Gerardo, as we look at the upcoming timeline for the Mediquant launch, I’m also keeping my eye on the Mid-October window for my Level 3 AI Assessment.

As my mentor for this, are there specific areas or documentation formats you think I should focus on right now that would simultaneously benefit the current DOM project?
