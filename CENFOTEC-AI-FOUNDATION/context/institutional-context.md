## Academic Policies

### Late Submission Policy

What is the policy for late assignments?

- Allowed? Yes
- Penalty if allowed: -15 points after 24 hours, -35 points between 24 and 48 hours, -100 after 48 hours
- Grace period: No grace without penalty
